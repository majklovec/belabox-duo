Branches carry descriptive names like rtmpsrc → demux. Edit any label in place.

—

A branch’s label is derived from its endpoints: the first stage (element, caps or name.pad link) and the last one, joined by →. Named elements use their name= property, so nvcompositor name=comp … ! mux. becomes comp → mux. Labels are editable on the block itself.
